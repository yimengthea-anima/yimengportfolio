14th ChelseaFilm Festival
Social Campaign 2026 · New York · Oct 14–18

Social media for the 14th Annual Chelsea Film Festival, New York’s international festival for independent film. I designed the content system and visual presentation across the festival’s channels, and created and delivered assets for publishing — announcements, reels, partner campaigns and more, in both image and video.

Edit Rules

  1. Key info firstThe date, name or brand is on screen within the opening seconds.
  2. Built for sound-offOn-screen type and captions carry the message without audio.
  3. Pace builds to a payoffQuick cuts gather energy toward one reveal.
  4. Safe-zone typeText kept clear of the platform UI at the top and bottom of the 9:16 frame.

Kocree✦

Commercial AnimationConcept / Product PromotionMotion Graphic Designer

01

Concept

A promotional film made for the pitch of Kocree, a music app. It introduces the app’s user personas as Muons — small musical spirits that each carry a listener’s taste — and walks through its core features across three worlds: an open world of Muons to explore, a deep-sea creativity dimension where Muons merge into new music, and a shared train journey where melodies meet in harmony with others. I was one of the motion graphic designers on the team.
